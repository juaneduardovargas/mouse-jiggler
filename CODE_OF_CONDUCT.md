# Code of Conduct

[Leer en español](docs/es/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Mouse Jiggler a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions clearly.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostility.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through the contact information available on [Juan Eduardo Vargas's GitHub profile](https://github.com/juaneduardovargas). Do not place sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled as consistently as possible.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject interactions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when behavior violates this policy. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern is prohibited.
